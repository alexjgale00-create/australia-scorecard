Homicide is the most reliably reported violent crime there is — unlike assault or theft, a body count is hard to under-report or redefine away, which makes it the most cross-country-comparable safety statistic available, even though it's a narrow slice of "personal safety" in the everyday sense. It's a floor, not a ceiling: a country can score well here and still have citizens who don't feel safe walking alone at night. Read alongside the rest of this dimension, not as a complete verdict on safety by itself.
