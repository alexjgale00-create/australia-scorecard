This gauge answers a different question from Living standards: not "how well-off is the average Australian" but "how much does Australia's economy weigh in the world, full stop." Australia's share has been drifting down for years — not because the Australian economy is shrinking, but because China, India, and other large emerging economies are growing faster than the world average. A falling share here is compatible with rising living standards at home; they're measuring different things.
