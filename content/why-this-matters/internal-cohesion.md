A country's trajectory isn't just economic — it also depends on whether people across the political spectrum can still work within the same institutions, or whether disagreement hardens into camps that no longer engage with each other. This gauge tracks political polarization: V-Dem's own definition is the extent to which a society is divided into hostile political camps that discourage interaction and cooperation across ideological lines. That's a different question from whether opinions differ — healthy democracies argue constantly — it's whether disagreement has calcified into a standing hostility that makes ordinary democratic bargaining harder. A country can score well on formal democratic institutions elsewhere on this Scorecard while polarization quietly erodes the informal trust those institutions depend on.
