PISA tests a representative sample of 15-year-olds across maths, reading, and science every three years, using the same instrument in every participating country. Today's 15-year-olds are tomorrow's workforce, which is why this gauge feeds directly into the productivity and innovation gauges elsewhere on this Scorecard. Australia's PISA average fell from 512 in 2012 to 497 in 2022 — a decline across all four measured cycles, not a single bad year.
