R&D spending as a share of GDP is a leading indicator of future productivity and export competitiveness — the innovation gauge is upstream of the productivity gauge elsewhere on this Scorecard, not a duplicate of it. Australia spends 1.86% of GDP on R&D against a peer median of 2.99% — a rank of 8th of 9 — and successive government reviews (including the Strategic Examination of R&D, whose final report — Ambitious Australia — was published in March 2026) have flagged this as a structural weakness. A country that under-invests in R&D today is choosing slower productivity growth a decade from now.
