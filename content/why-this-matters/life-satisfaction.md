Every other gauge on this site measures something about a country from the outside — GDP, homicide rates, air pollution. Life satisfaction asks the people who actually live there. It's the closest this Scorecard comes to asking Australians directly whether their country is working for them, rather than inferring it from proxies. It's also genuinely different evidence from a hard statistic — a self-report, not a measurement — which is exactly why it carries the site's "Survey-based" tag rather than being presented as equivalent to a homicide count or a GDP figure.
