### Why this matters (full text)
*Source: `content/why-this-matters/external-position.md`*

```
[DRAFT — edit freely]

Its own gauge page is unusually candid about a limitation: a real search across five candidate sources (Gallup's own broader item, WVS Wave 8, Pew, Ipsos, Edelman) found nothing with full 9-peer coverage, so this gauge runs on Gallup's own Migrant Acceptance Index — its two most recent freely published waves are 2016/17 and 2019. That absence is itself worth knowing, not just the number.
